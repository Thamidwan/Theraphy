What you get when you refer to OA

  • Regular progress & outcome reports — structured updates on your patient's progress against the goals set at discharge.
  • A seat at our MDT case discussions — an open invitation to join the conversation on shared patients, so your clinical input stays part of the plan.
  • A direct line to the treating therapist — no call centre, no run-around; you speak directly to the OA clinician managing the case.
  • A discharge summary back to you — a full summary at the end of the programme, closing the loop on the referral.

The Five Disciplines

Whatever your recovery needs, it's under one roof.

Presented in the same fixed order as the OA brochure — Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy, Clinical Psychology, Speech Therapy, Dietetics. Each card carries a real photograph of an OA practitioner working with a patient in that discipline.

1 Physiotherapy

Restoring movement, strength, and physical function after injury, surgery, or stroke.

2 Occupational Therapy

Getting you back to the daily tasks that matter: home, work, and independence.

3 Clinical Psychology

Evidence-based mental health support, because recovery is never only physical.

4 Speech Therapy

Rebuilding communication and swallowing function after stroke, TBI, or neurological illness.

5 Dietetics

Personalised, practical nutrition for recovery and chronic condition management.

You don't have to recover alone.

OA runs structured group sessions for patients with shared conditions — peer support, shared accountability, and professional guidance, with light meals and refreshments on site.
