# Image credits & status

**These are interim stock photographs**, sourced to fill the layout for review. Per the
OA Website Brief (§3.5 Imagery direction and §10 Appendix), every one of these must be
replaced before launch with **real, consented OA photography** — real patients, real
staff, real environments, and one genuine photo per discipline of an OA practitioner
working with a patient.

## Replacing an image

Keep the same filename and drop the new file in this folder — no HTML or CSS changes
are needed. Recommended: landscape, at least 1400px wide, JPEG optimised to
under ~250 KB. The five discipline photos must stay in the brochure's fixed order
(Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy, Clinical Psychology, Speech Therapy, Dietetics).
